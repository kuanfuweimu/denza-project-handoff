Lines and Strength
Composed of a dense arrangement of lines, the carbon fiber trim is both compact and lightweight.
These intersecting lines not only define the structure but also evoke the distinctive, inherent strength of
carbon fiber—supporting the dynamic, surging stance of a lightweight sports car.
